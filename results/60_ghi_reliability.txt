Why two domains give negative alpha
==============================================================================
VH_um vs CD_um: r = +0.522 (n = 311)
BWG_g vs FCR: r = +0.046 (n = 116)

Both are DIFFERENCE scores, for which alpha is undefined, but for different
reasons and they should not be described together:
  Morphology  VH_um and CD_um correlate strongly and positively (+0.52), so
              their difference is dominated by measurement noise. This is why
              the morphology domain contributes least to the composite (r =
              0.646) despite having the most observations (n = 323).
  Performance BWG_g and FCR are essentially uncorrelated (+0.05). The two
              markers carry near-independent information; alpha is slightly
              negative only because direction correction flips the sign.
NOTE: use BWG_g (weight GAIN), the marker named in the index definition.
BW_g (absolute weight) correlates with FCR at +0.49 and is a different column.
